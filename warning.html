Security Awareness Check

You just scanned a random QR code at a public networking event. While this interaction is safe, you've executed a digital command based entirely on implicit trust.

In the wild, Bad actors weaponize this exact vector—known as "Quishing" (QR Phishing)—to cleanly bypass security filters often by just placing a QR code sticker over a genuine QR code. A malicious QR code can effortlessly direct targets to fraud websites (like a public parking website) where they can harvest credentials and payment infomation.
